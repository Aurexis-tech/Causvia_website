01 // The Engine

One continuous run

Not a suite of tools — a single engine. You go from who you are to a live, in-market product in one pass, and you watch each step actually work. No stage is sold off or spun out.

02 // The Moat

Sourced, not simulated

Most tools hand you a confident score built from your own words. Aurexis only shows a number when it comes from a real fetch, scan, or probe — and honestly marks what it can’t know. Empty tiles say “no data yet” instead of inventing traction.
